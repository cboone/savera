# Build Savera, a physically modelled peti

## Context

Savera is a macOS audio plugin containing a physically modelled Indian hand harmonium, the peti: a small pressure-blown free-reed box whose player pumps a bellows with one hand and plays a keyboard with the other. It is authored once as a CLAP instrument in Zig and projected outward to an Audio Unit by clap-wrapper, so it runs in Logic Pro as a software instrument and in REAPER and Bitwig as a CLAP.

The project has two goals that pull in different directions and this plan serves both. The first is realism: the reed, the air supply and the wooden box are modelled from the free-reed acoustics literature and calibrated against recordings, so that what comes out sounds like a peti rather than like an organ patch. The second, and the owner's ultimate one, is a highly modulatable synthesizer with that particular sound and a set of affordances specific to the instrument: continuous bellows pressure as the loudness control, a shared air supply that sags under chords, keys that are valves rather than velocity sensors, drones, an octave coupler, a scale changer, and past the acoustic instrument, continuous pitch and microtonality, which no real free reed can do. Realism is the foundation; modulation is the point.

The brainstorm this plan grew from is preserved verbatim at `docs/design/peti-physical-model-brainstorm.md`. It was written as a map of territory rather than a specification, it deliberately scoped out plugin hosting, parameters and build infrastructure, and several of its physics claims did not survive ground-truthing. The findings section below records what changed and why.

The sibling projects [`fosforo`](https://github.com/cboone/fosforo) (`~/Development/fosforo`) and [`springer`](https://github.com/cboone/springer) (`~/Development/springer`) are the precedent for everything structural: Zig 0.16.0, CLAP authored directly against the C API, clap-wrapper projecting outward to an AUv2, a pure core behind a comptime-enforced seam, stable parameter ids, an audio thread that never allocates, and a documentation and verification culture that this plan adopts wholesale. Where savera is the first project in the family to need something, such as a bounded per-sample solver, a fixed internal rate, a voice engine, or a modulation surface, this plan says so and records the decision as an ADR rather than inheriting one.

This document is the master plan and is permanent: it stays at `docs/plans/` for the life of the project and is never moved to `done/`. The `commit` and `pr` skills do not yet implement the permanent-plan exemption of catalog issue #347; they move a plan from `todo/` or from the `docs/plans/` root once they judge its work complete, which this file will read as after the last phase and which any phase PR that edits it could misjudge, so `AGENTS.md` states that this file never moves. Per-phase plans are separate dated files under `docs/plans/todo/` that move to `docs/plans/done/` when their PR merges, and each phase appends a `### Phase N outcomes (complete)` subsection here in the same PR.

## What we are building

A note played on the keyboard opens a pallet valve over one key's reeds. The reeds sit in a pressurised bank chamber fed from a spring-loaded reservoir that the bellows charge, so every sounding reed draws on one shared supply. Loudness lives entirely in the bellows pressure, which the player supplies continuously from an expression pedal, a breath controller, aftertouch, or a synthesized pumping generator that pumps for them, free-running or locked to the host tempo. Pressing a key partway restricts the air path, so the note comes in quieter, fades in rather than starting, and flattens slightly, which is the mechanism players use to fake the pitch ornaments the instrument cannot make. Adding notes drains the reservoir faster, so chords sag and thin. Releasing a key closes the pallet over the reed, and what the listener hears after that is the reed's own ring-down through the closing valve, longer for bass reeds than for treble.

The target instrument is a Kolkata-style three-bank peti with Palitana reeds: a brass-and-bronze alloy softer than the German reeds of a Lahore instrument and the Panipat brass of a Delhi one, in a teak body, with a 42-key keyboard from C3 to F6 (MIDI 48 to 89), the male bank at written pitch under the lowest key, the bass bank an octave below and the female bank an octave above, five drone stops on the black-key pitches, an octave coupler and a scale changer. The first release is that instrument reduced to one bank: the male reeds across the keyboard, the reservoir and bellows, key depth, the five drones, and the wooden enclosure. The full instrument adds the bass and female banks, the coupler with its air cost and click, the scale changer, the mechanical tremolo flap, and presets for the Delhi and Lahore instruments that differ in reed stiffness and damping, cabinet, coupler default and drone count rather than in registration alone. Beyond the acoustic instrument come the things only a model can do: internal modulators routed to physical quantities, host modulation of every continuous parameter down to the individual note, continuous pitch glides that move a reed along a physically consistent family of reeds, tuning tables for the 22 shruti, and the reed and reservoir parameters unlocked past their real ranges.

Every physical quantity that the engine reads per sample is a parameter, and every such parameter is modulatable. The model is the synthesizer; there is no separate synth engine bolted on.

`features` is `{ instrument, synthesizer }`. Two positive controls guard the AU type, chosen so that neither is disarmed by the other. First, a unit test that `features[0]` equals `CLAP_PLUGIN_FEATURE_INSTRUMENT` as restated in `src/clap/c.zig`, because the macro does not survive preprocessing. Second, a CI step that reads the type out of the artifact rather than out of a log: `plutil -extract AudioComponents.0.type raw` on the built component's `Info.plist` must print `aumu`, proven by planting a wrong value and confirming the step goes red. `AUV2_INSTRUMENT_TYPE "aumu"` is passed to `make_clapfirst_plugins` as fosforo passes `aufx`, and the plan records that this short-circuits clap-wrapper's `features[0]` mapping entirely: the build helper checks the explicit type first, so its "can't determine instrument type" warning cannot fire in the shipping configuration and no grep for it would be a control on anything.

## Build architecture

Follows fosforo's shape closely enough that `build.zig`, `cmake/CMakeLists.txt`, `macos/Info.plist`, `packaging/distribution.xml`, and most of `scripts/` start as adaptations rather than new work. Springer's Phase 0 plan lists the same set and the same origin.

- `build.zig` is the primary build and the task runner. Four artifacts from one module factory: an object file `savera_impl` for CMake, a dynamic library exporting `clap_entry` and packaged as `Savera.clap`, the offline render harness executable, and the offline host harness executable.
- The object file is built with `b.addObject` and installed through `addInstallFileWithDir(impl.getEmittedBin(), ...)`, rather than an `addLibrary(.static)` archive, because Zig 0.16's archive writer pads member names to four bytes rather than eight, so whether the object member inside a `.a` lands on the eight-byte boundary Apple's linker requires depends on the size of the symbol table: springer's archive was refused with `ld: 64-bit mach-o member '...' not 8-byte aligned` and its `build.zig` records the two rejected alternatives beside `addObject`, while fosforo's archive happens to align and links. An object removes the archive format from the question. Revisit when the Zig pin moves.
- `cmake/CMakeLists.txt` exists only to run clap-wrapper and emit `Savera.component`. `set(SAVERA_FORMATS CLAP AUV2)`. It sets `CMAKE_OSX_DEPLOYMENT_TARGET`, `CMAKE_CXX_STANDARD 17` and `CLAP_WRAPPER_DOWNLOAD_DEPENDENCIES TRUE`, which clap-wrapper does not set for a consumer. The wrapper downloads its own CLAP headers; a configure-time check asserts the version it resolved matches the tag `build.zig.zon` pins, so the Zig side's layout assertions guard the header set the wrapper actually uses.
- clap-wrapper is pinned by commit to `1cca996e96f29ab2be7ae9f8cfe532bbc92e1dd6` (2026-08-08, the v0.16.0 release) or later, for the same three PRs springer records: full AUv2 MIDI 1.0 and 2.0 input with dialect negotiation, the silent facade, and cleared silence buffers. The reason is recorded in the CMake file the way fosforo records why it pins a commit rather than a tag.
- The wrapper's generated plist is rewritten before signing by scripts with a `--check` mode, adapted from fosforo's `cmake/narrow-au-resource-usage` and `cmake/set-au-display-name`, and CI asserts the result independently.
- Deployment target macOS 11.0, stated in `build.zig`, `cmake/CMakeLists.txt`, `macos/Info.plist`, and `packaging/distribution.xml`, which must stay in step.
- Ad-hoc code signing is a build step, `/usr/bin/codesign` by absolute path, `--timestamp --options runtime` added only when a real identity is supplied through `SAVERA_SIGNING_IDENTITY`.
- Build provenance stamped at configure time via `b.runAllowFail` with two separate `git rev-parse` calls, read back by `scripts/read-provenance`, and logged from `plugin.init` so the linker does not strip the marker as unreferenced. Savera is the third plugin installing into the same `~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins` tree as fosforo and springer, so the multi-worktree hazard the siblings record is live from the first install.
- Every `install-*` step builds exactly what it installs, copies it through the one `scripts/install-plugins` implementation, and prints the hash and provenance of what landed. Every other step stays in the worktree.
- The unit suite runs in three additive modes, `test`, `test-safe` and `test-release`, because the shipped CLAP is ReleaseFast and `std.debug.assert` is gone there. Every trust boundary refuses with a runtime `if`. A solver whose convergence were guarded by an assert would hide its failure in exactly the build that ships.
- fosforo's early return for non-macOS hosts exists because zig-objc's build function panics off Darwin. Savera has no GUI and so no zig-objc, links no Apple framework, and its CLAP dependency has no build function, so `zig build render`, `zig build test` and `zig build smoke` all build on Linux; the non-macOS return guards only signing, the `install-*` steps and the CMake step, and sits below everything else as fosforo's does. CI's Zig job runs on `macos-latest`, as fosforo's does, with `run-cross-compile: false`; the Linux path is what lets the oracle row run beside the Ubuntu `python` job.

## The instrument model

### The reed

Millot and Baumann's minimal model. The reed tip displacement is a single-degree-of-freedom oscillator standing in for the first transverse cantilever mode, driven by the pressure difference across it:

```text
ζ̈ + (ω₀ / Q) ζ̇ + ω₀² ζ = μ Δp        μ = S_r / M,  M = K / ω₀²
```

The jet through the instantaneous opening obeys a quasi-stationary Bernoulli relation, `Δp = ½ ρ v_j²`, and the total flow is the sum of a pumped component `S_r ζ̇` and a jet component `α S_u(h) v_j`, where `S_u` is the useful section, the escape area as a function of the tip's position relative to the plate. The reed is blown closed, Fletcher's `(−,+)` configuration: it plays slightly below its natural frequency, amplitude depends strongly on pressure, and frequency depends on it only slightly.

Four things this plan fixes that the brainstorm left open:

- **The aperture is pluggable and its baseline is stated.** The baseline is Millot's own definition (his equation 9) with the measured geometry of Puranik and Scavone's B5 harmonium reed: 16 mm by 2 mm by 0.4 mm, an initial tip offset Δy of 0.1 mm, Young's modulus 125 GPa, density 8490 kg/m³. Millot's equation also needs the side clearance `h_min`, which no harmonium source publishes; it is a fitted parameter in Phase 2, bracketed by Millot's harmonica 50 µm and the Euphonics harmonica's 0.2 mm, and its sensitivity is reported with the aperture comparison. Puranik's simplified aperture and Tarnopolsky's form are implemented alongside the baseline in the harness, and the Phase 2 report compares all three, expecting the roughly fivefold overpressure spread Puranik reported. Tables are over geometry only, `S_u(h)` and `dS_u/dh`, and the chain-rule factor from the bilinear step is computed separately, so a change of rate or a modulation of `ω₀` or `Q` never leaves a stale table.
- **The Bernoulli relation is regularised.** `v_j = sqrt(2Δp/ρ)` has infinite slope at zero pressure difference, which Newton crosses at every onset and every sag to threshold. A branch linear in `Δp` cannot match both value and slope at a crossover, so the regularised branch on `[0, Δp_c]` is a cubic Hermite with zero value and finite slope at the origin and matched value and slope at `Δp_c`; the alternative, value-only matching with an accepted slope jump, is measured against it in the harness. Above `Δp_c` the relation is odd-symmetric, `v_j = sign(Δp) sqrt(2|Δp| / ρ)`, because the pressure difference reverses at onset and at sag to threshold. The crossover is a harness experiment and its value is recorded with ADR 0010.
- **The oscillator sits behind a reed seam that exports what the solver needs.** `reed.zig` exposes a state, a pressure load in, a tip position and velocity out, and the two sensitivities the solver's Jacobian needs, `dζ/dΔp` and `dζ̇/dΔp` for the current step. For the bilinear single-mode reed those are the two bilinear coefficients; for a modal reed they are the mode-shape-weighted sums of the per-mode coefficients; for the clamped bar they come from its implicit step. Exporting them is what lets the oscillator be replaced without touching the flow, aperture, solver or air code. A two- or three-mode modal reed, projecting the same pressure load onto the first transverse modes of a clamped-free beam, fits the seam; so does the clamped bar of FA2023, at roughly forty times the reed-update cost before the flow solve from its 40 grid sections. ADR 0009 records the single-mode reed's validity ceiling near 1 kPa and the attack modes it cannot produce as named deficits.
- **The bank's reed table is generated, not hand-set.** Exactly one harmonium reed has published geometry. Every other reed of a bank is generated from that B5 anchor by the cantilever family `family.zig` implements, holding width and material fixed and scaling thickness and length along `f ∝ t / L²`, with per-reed Q from the corpus ring-downs and a per-reed tuning offset as the only hand-set quantities. The generated table is comptime in `tables.zig`, sized at 54 reeds per bank (42 keys plus six either side for the scale changer), and its deviation from real instruments, where bass reeds are weighted rather than scaled, is a recorded deficit.

Calibration starts from two bracketing damping values, Millot's harmonica Q of 95 and the Q of about 650 that FA2023's beam damping implies for the B5 reed, which give ring-down time constants by `τ = Q / (π f₀)` of 31 ms and 209 ms at 990 Hz. It anchors absolute behaviour on the only pressure-annotated harmonium data that exists: the B5 reed's geometry and its two LDV pressure points, and Puranik's 200 to 1000 Pa reed-chamber trace. Per-reed Q is then fitted from the ring-downs in the corpus.

### The air path

The chain, upstream to downstream:

```text
hand → external bellows → flap → reservoir → stop orifice → bank chamber → reed → near-field pipe → pallet orifice → cabinet
```

| Stage            | State it carries                                                              |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hand             | The player's pumping input, from a controller or the pump generator           |
| External bellows | A flow source                                                                 |
| Flap             | One-way, from the external bellows into the reservoir                         |
| Reservoir        | A volume state under a spring law, with a leak                                |
| Stop orifice     | Per bank, area from the stop                                                  |
| Bank chamber     | Per bank, Millot's compressible volume `V1`, shared by every reed in the bank |
| Reed             | Per key, per bank                                                             |
| Near-field pipe  | Per key, the inertance `L2 / S2` of the hole in the reed board                |
| Pallet orifice   | Per key, area from key depth                                                  |
| Cabinet          | The enclosure filter                                                          |

The reservoir is the internal spring-loaded bellows every repair source describes: a volume state under a spring law that sets the static working pressure, charged through a one-way flap from the external bellows the hand pumps, and losing air through a leak and through every open reed. That is what makes chords sag and what makes a single note hold for ten to twenty seconds while a chord holds for less. The brainstorm's compliance-as-air-compressibility cannot do this: an 18 litre box has an adiabatic compliance of `V / (ρ₀ c₀²)`, which is `0.018 / 1.4e5`, about `1.3e-7 m³/Pa`, so it stores about 130 millilitres at 1 kPa, roughly one second of one reed's draw and an order of magnitude short of a ten to twenty second hold. Air compressibility stays in the model as the chamber term; it is not the reservoir. Each bank has its own sealed chamber behind its stop, and Millot's upstream volume `V1` is that chamber. The pallet is a downstream orifice whose area follows key depth, sitting at the far end of the short near-field pipe that Puranik's `L2` and `S2` describe and that the reed board's hole is. Whether that pipe belongs upstream of the reed (Millot's topology, which Puranik reused) or downstream (where the target instrument's geometry puts it), and whether the chamber's `V1` is the single-reed figure or scales with the reeds in the bank, are Phase 2 findings: the harness implements both and the plan records which the calibration supports.

Starting values, from Puranik and Scavone FA2023 Table 1: `V1 = 1.3e-3 m³`, `L2 = 50 mm`, `S2 = 6.4e-5 m²`, `ρ₀ = 1.1769 kg/m³`. Millot and Baumann 2007 Table 3 gives the harmonica geometry for comparison: a feed area of 30 mm², a chamber section of 800 mm², `L2 = 20 mm`, `S2 = 25 mm²`, with `V1` between 12 and 64 cm³. FA2023 states its `V1`, `S2` and `L2` are arbitrary in that model, so they are starting values and the calibration pass sets them.

Because every reed in a bank sees the same chamber pressure, and every bank sees the same reservoir, the reeds are coupled through those two states and nowhere else. The shared chamber's ripple is about plus or minus 20 Pa on 450 Pa in Puranik's adapted model. No additional coupling term between reeds is added, and no mode-locking behaviour is a requirement or a gate. A chamber resonance near a reed frequency is a measured effect, in reed cells two orders of magnitude smaller than a shared bank chamber: Cottingham reports the threshold raised by an order of magnitude and a 344 Hz reed pulled to 312 Hz. The model produces whatever resonance the calibrated chamber and its open pipes have, since the chamber compliance and the pipe inertance are both state, and the Helmholtz choking invariant in the oracle checks that the behaviour is reproduced rather than tuned away.

Calibration targets, each with its source: reed-chamber pressure between 200 and 1000 Pa with sharp rises on strokes and exponential decay between them (Puranik and Scavone, POMA 2022, Fig. 5); five to eight seconds of leak-down with every stop closed and ten to twenty seconds for a single held note (Chuck White's repair notes and the Bhava builder page); a soundbox of about 18 litres and an external bellows of about 2.75 litres (one engineering CAD paper reached only through a search excerpt, so a weak figure to be re-derived from the model's own hold times); eight to twelve strokes per minute in ordinary playing (an instructional site, unverified). Per-reed flow of zero to 160 millilitres per second is Millot's harmonica simulation rather than a harmonium measurement, so it is an order-of-magnitude plausibility bound and never a gate; the gated per-reed flow is whatever reproduces the measured leak-down and hold times against the bellows supply.

### Keys, coupler, drones, scale changer, tremolo

A key is a valve. Its depth, from a per-note pressure expression, polyphonic aftertouch, or the note-on itself when the host has neither, sets the pallet orifice area through a trajectory whose rate comes from velocity. Full depth is the normal note; partial depth gives less flow and a fade-in rather than an onset, and it pulls the pitch down: the figure Cottingham's group measured is up to 2.5 Hz on a 96 Hz reed at a quarter depression, about 45 cents at that pitch (Coyle, Behrens and Cottingham 2009, CCRMA 2013 slide 27, on what the slides' context indicates is an accordion reed), and no harmonium figure exists. The pull is modelled as an acoustic load, the partly closed pallet changing the near-field inertance and end impedance the reed sees, because a reduced pressure difference alone would move the single-mode reed's pitch the other way. Velocity also sets the key noise level. Nothing else reads velocity.

Because the pallet is downstream, closing it stops the flow and closes the reed's path into the cabinet together, so the audible release is the product of the reed's mechanical ring-down and the closing pallet's transmission. Phase 5 measures both against the per-key release samples in the corpus and records which dominates; the steal reserve, the `NOTE_END` threshold and the perceptual tier's ring-down target are sized from the audible decay, while `τ = Q / (π f₀)` remains an analytic invariant on the reed state alone.

The coupler is a second pallet per key, an octave below or above, engaged by a stepped parameter and never changed mid-phrase on the real instrument. It doubles the air cost and adds a click; both are modelled. The five drones are stops that open a hole over an unkeyed reed, on the black-key pitches by default and retunable. Whether a drone on a three-bank instrument sounds one reed or one per open bank is a Phase 8 finding, since nothing before the second bank depends on the answer and the maker literature disagrees; the capacity below assumes the worst case. The scale changer is a key-to-reed offset over fixed reeds, from minus six to plus six semitones, so fingering is preserved and the reeds are not retuned. The tremolo stop is a weighted flap on the bank chamber that lifts under flow and drops under its own weight, giving a square-ish amplitude pulse; it works only with the bank's other stops closed and it is modelled that way.

### The cabinet

Following Puranik and Scavone: the reed model is the excitation and the wooden enclosure is a filter. Their all-pole filter estimated by iterative adaptive inverse filtering from a recorded note had 49 coefficients and eight or nine peaks below about 10 kHz, approximated by ten biquads. Savera ships the same shape as movable peaks with per-instrument presets, fitted from the best-provenanced recording available for each target (see The calibration corpus), with the jali cover open and closed as two presets and a morph between them. The excitation is the derivative of total flow, pumped plus jet, which is what Puranik convolves; the choice matters because flow versus its derivative changes the spectral tilt by 6 dB per octave and every oracle comparison depends on it. The far field of a free reed is dipolar; the monopole approximation is deliberate and recorded in ADR 0014, and a dipole radiation model is deferred until the filter and recordings exist to judge it against. The output port is stereo with both channels carrying the same monopole signal at first release; the jali morph and any later dipole model are what would decorrelate them, and the port is declared with two channels now because channel count cannot change without a deactivated rescan.

### The solver

Millot's scheme, adapted to savera's topology. Backward Euler for the air path; the bilinear transform with frequency pre-warping for the reed oscillator, following Gazengel. Millot's single unknown is the reed-side pressure in a topology where the reed discharges to atmosphere, so that one quantity is both the reed's downstream node and the pressure across it, and his residual is the upstream chamber's mass balance with one reed. Savera's topology differs twice: the pallet sits downstream of the reed, so a near-field pressure exists between reed and pallet, the reed's load and jet are set by the chamber pressure minus that near-field pressure, and the pallet discharges it to the cabinet; and a bank has many reeds on one chamber.

Per internal sample and per reed, one nonlinear equation in that reed's near-field pressure, whose residual is flow continuity at the near-field node: reed flow in, pumped plus jet through the chamber-to-near-field difference with the chamber pressure held at its previous-sample value, equals pallet flow out through the near-field-to-cabinet difference with the pallet's area from key depth, with the near-field pipe's inertance carried as state between them. With the pallet fully open the near-field pressure sits at the cabinet's and the equation reduces to Millot's; with it partly closed the near-field pressure rises, which is where the loudness reduction and the acoustic-load pull come from. Two square-root terms sit in one residual, one on each side of the node, and the Phase 2 harness measures whether it stays single-rooted over the bracket, which is the physical range between the cabinet pressure and the chamber pressure. The bank chamber's own mass balance, `(V1 / (ρ₀ c₀²)) Fs (p₁[n] − p₁[n−1]) = u_in[n] − Σⱼ uⱼ[n]`, is an explicit update applied after the per-reed solves, and the reservoir's volume state likewise. The harness single-reed model runs the same lagged chamber update the engine does, so the two discretisations agree and the lockstep tier is meaningful; Millot's simultaneous scheme is run once in the harness to bound the lag's effect and the figure is recorded.

The shipping solver is fixed-K bracketed Newton. `K` is a compile-time constant measured in the harness; the working assumption for budgets is 4. A Newton step that leaves the bracket is replaced by a bisection step, and an iteration that reaches `K` carries its last iterate and increments an overrun counter. That counter is a relaxed atomic incremented on the audio thread and drained on the main thread in the parameter flush, and every other cross-thread datum follows the same rule: single writer, relaxed atomic, drained on the main thread. Savera starts no thread of its own, so no Thread Sanitizer harness is needed; a source canary asserts that nothing under `src/` spawns one, and the parameter store keeps a main-thread copy that `state.save` reads while the audio thread's copy is updated from events, so the two never race. This satisfies ADR 0007's bounded-work rule by construction rather than by hope. The harness carries two modes: converge-to-tolerance, for discovery, and the identical fixed-K mode, and it reports the fixed-K error per event class (steady state, attack, pressure step, release) so the choice of `K` is a measured trade rather than a guess.

The explicit coupling is stable while the chamber's time constant `τ_c = C / Σⱼ (duⱼ / dp)` stays long against the internal sample period, where `C = V1 / (ρ₀ c₀²)` and the sum runs over the bank's sounding reeds, so the margin shrinks with polyphony and as the pressure difference falls toward the regularised branch, where `du/dp` is largest. The render harness carries a bordered-Newton reference mode over all of a bank's reeds, in Zig behind `zig build render` as ADR 0015 requires, and Phase 5 checks the explicit scheme against it at the calibrated values and at the bank's worst-case reed count before the engine commits to it, with the result recorded as an amendment to ADR 0010. Explicit coupling makes reeds independent within a sample, which is what permits `@Vector` lanes across reeds; the lanes vectorise the arithmetic only, since neither Zig nor Apple's NEON has a gather, so each lane's `S_u(h)` and `dS_u/dh` lookups are scalar loads into an array that is then coerced. Phase 2 measures the lane arm against the scalar baseline on sixteen independent copies of the single reed at constant pressure, which needs no coupling, so the lane figure is known before `K` and the rate are frozen.

Numerics that the audio thread must get right: solver state and tables in `f64`, output in `f32`; the solver module in `.strict` float mode so the residual is the same in every build and the oracle comparison is meaningful; flush-to-zero set through bit 24 of the ARM64 floating-point control register with save and restore around `process()`, because a reed rings to minus 60 dB over seconds and reaches subnormals on every release, and `@setFloatMode` does not touch that register; a runtime check the optimizer cannot fold, verified by plant.

### The internal rate and the resampler

Backward Euler's first-order damping makes threshold pressure, growth rate and the pressure-to-pitch coefficient depend on the internal rate, so a host-rate-times-factor design sounds different in a 44.1 kHz session and a 96 kHz one and defeats the oracle. The model therefore runs at a fixed internal rate, chosen in Phase 2 by measuring the convergence of those three quantities and the folded-back aliasing at two, four, eight and sixteen times 48 kHz, then frozen as a compile-time constant. The working assumption for budgets is 192 kHz. A rational polyphase resampler carries the internal rate to the host rate, its ratios for the 44.1 and 48 kHz families tabulated at `activate` into storage sized for the largest, and its latency reported through `clap.latency`. `activate` refuses host rates outside the tabulated set with a runtime `if`, and it also sizes every internal-rate buffer from the host's maximum frame count times the internal-to-host ratio times the reed capacity, from a fixed-size arena taken once; `process` refuses a block larger than the declared maximum with a runtime `if` and counts it, and a test asserts the arena's end index returns to zero on every block, as fosforo does.

Events arrive with frame offsets at the host rate and the block is split at every event offset. Host frame `k` maps to internal position `k R` where `R` is the fixed rational ratio (640/147 at 44.1 kHz against 192 kHz), so a segment's internal sample count is the difference of the floors at its two ends and varies by one from segment to segment; the resampler's phase accumulator and polyphase history are engine state that persist across segments and across blocks and are reset only by `reset()` and `activate()`. Parameter and expression values are applied at the segment boundary. A `zig build smoke` case drives one block at 44.1 kHz with an event on every frame and asserts the block emits exactly the host frame count and that the output equals the same events delivered in one unsplit block, which is the planted-defect arm for per-segment rounding. Continuous inputs such as bellows pressure are smoothed at the internal rate with a time constant that is itself a parameter, because a fixed smoothing ramp is exactly what makes digital accordions miss the first strokes of a bellows shake.

`clap.tail` reports the longest remaining ring-down in host samples, derived from the slowest sounding reed's measured decay plus the cabinet filter's, and a value at or above `INT32_MAX` (infinite) whenever any drone stop is open or the pump generator is running, so a host does not truncate a bounce. `process` returns `CLAP_PROCESS_CONTINUE` while any reed, drone or reservoir state is non-quiet, `CLAP_PROCESS_TAIL` while only ring-down remains, and never `CLAP_PROCESS_SLEEP` with a drone open. Through clap-wrapper v0.16.0 the Audio Unit reports no tail regardless, because `SupportsTail()` is hard-coded false while `GetTailTime()` is implemented; that is a recorded wrapper deficit, not a plugin one, and the Logic bounce step in the by-hand protocol records what it does.

### Voices, capacity and the CPU budget

A voice is a held key. Each voice fans out to one reed per engaged bank, times two when the coupler is engaged, and each drone is a fixed slot outside the voice table. Every reed occupies one of a fixed number of reed slots, and the derivation lives in one place with the number:

```text
keyboard                  42        C3 to F6, MIDI 48 to 89; notes outside are refused and counted
reeds per bank            54        42 keys plus six either side for the scale changer
keys held (cap)            8
banks                      3        bass, male, female
coupler                    2        engaged doubles the fan-out
reeds per voice, worst     6
drone stops                5
drone reeds, worst        15        one per open bank per drone, pending the Phase 8 finding
worst-case demand         63        8 × 6 + 15
derived demand            33        three keys, coupler, three banks, five drones each sounding one reed per bank
reed slots (capacity)     32        with a steal policy; the derived demand exceeds it by one
CPU gate                  16        sounding reeds within a quarter of one performance core
CPU stretch               32        measured and reported, not gated
```

The steal policy is written down beside the capacity: a new reed takes a free slot, then the oldest slot that is released and ringing, then the oldest held slot of the same voice class; drones are never stolen; a reserve of slots sized from the measured audible decay keeps released reeds audible under normal playing. Because the derived kirtan and qawwali demand exceeds the capacity, stealing is a shipped behaviour in the full instrument rather than an overflow path, and Phase 8 gates it on a score whose demand exceeds the slots. `clap.voice-info` reports `voice_count` and `voice_capacity` of 8, the key cap, with `SUPPORTS_OVERLAPPING_NOTES`, since Bitwig's voice stack stays mono-mapped without it. When the last reed of a voice falls below the audible-decay threshold the engine emits `CLAP_EVENT_NOTE_END`, which polyphonic modulation in the host requires and which the ring-down makes mandatory: a voice outlives its note-off by up to several hundred milliseconds.

The CPU gate is measured in ReleaseFast at 48 kHz with 64-frame buffers on the development machine with nothing else running: sixteen sounding reeds within a quarter of one performance core. Until three banks exist the held keys and drones alone reach thirteen on one bank, and `zig build smoke -Dreeds=16` reaches sixteen sounding reeds through the plugin's real path by note overlap, since a released reed keeps its slot and sounds until it has rung down; sixteen reeds through the keyboard with all banks open is re-measured in Phase 8. An operation count gives roughly 250 to 500 cycles per reed per internal sample: about eight multiply-adds for the bilinear reed update, then per Newton iteration an interpolated table lookup for `S_u` and `dS_u/dh`, two square roots, one division and about thirty further operations, at `K` of 4. At 192 kHz that puts sixteen reeds between roughly a fifth and two fifths of one performance core at the clock rates Apple's performance cores run, so the gate is reachable only at the low end and Phase 2's measurement decides whether `K`, the internal rate, or lanes across reeds must move while the decision is still open. Thirty-two reeds is measured and reported as a stretch figure. The hybrid engine under Explicitly deferred reopens only if this gate fails.

Flags, ranges and defaults are decided per parameter at declaration and cannot be cheaply changed afterwards, so the policy is stated now and the per-parameter values live in the Phase 3 plan. Every continuous physical parameter the engine could read per sample carries `IS_AUTOMATABLE | IS_MODULATABLE`. Those that are per-voice in the engine, which are the reed and key groups, additionally carry the per-note, per-key and per-channel modulation and automation flags. Structural parameters such as Range, Bellows Source, Pump Mode, Key Depth Source, Coupler, Cabinet Preset, Registration, Temperament and Steal Mode are `IS_STEPPED`, several `IS_ENUM`, and none modulatable. Every continuous physical parameter is declared at the widest range the engine will ever accept, the beyond-acoustic range included, with the realistic value as its default; the stepped Range parameter clamps the engine to the acoustic range at runtime, so unlocking the extended range in Phase 9 changes no declared bound. Setting a modulation flag the engine does not yet honour is harmless, since the engine ignores `PARAM_MOD` for that id until it can act on it; clearing one later is the expensive operation. The heard value is `value + mod`, a polyphonic modulation already includes the monophonic signal, and no modulation amount is ever written into state.

Internal modulation is what works in every host. Two LFOs with free and tempo-synced rates, two envelopes triggered per note, an eight-slot routing matrix whose targets are the modulatable parameters above, and four macros, all landing in Phase 9. Bellows pressure is the first target and the pump generator, which ships in the first release, is itself a modulator with a stroke shape, so "warbly, pulsing" uneven pumping and the tremolo flap are both reachable. The matrix's outputs enter the same `value + mod` sum as host modulation, so the two compose.

The bellows parameter is fed by whichever source the player has: an expression pedal, a breath controller, channel pressure, the modwheel, or the pump generator, mapped through the plugin's own CC learn. In every dialect and every host those controllers arrive as raw MIDI, so `midi.zig` parses them from Phase 1, and a CC-driven change is pushed back to the host as a `PARAM_VALUE` event flagged `DONT_RECORD` so automation and live control never conflict. The learned map starts from CC 11 to Bellows with CC 2 as the alternate, and it is carried in state.

The first release's state is the magic `SVRA`, a `u32` version, every parameter's plain value by id, and the CC learn map as parameter id, controller number and channel triples; drone pitches, the scale-changer offset and tuning selections are parameters and need no separate field. Unknown trailing bytes are tolerated and unknown ids are skipped; new fields append. The format is versioned from the first build, as fosforo's is, so a project saved before parameters exist still loads later.

Presets are delivered two ways, and the plan says so before Phase 3 assigns ids. For CLAP hosts, a preset-discovery factory under the provider id in the identifiers table plus `clap.preset-load`, with the preset files installed beside the bundle. For Logic, neither reaches the Audio Unit, because clap-wrapper v0.16.0 implements no preset extension; the AU path is the stepped, non-modulatable Registration parameter in the Banks group, whose id is reserved now, whose values name Kolkata, Delhi and Lahore, and which sets the other bank and cabinet parameters when it changes, plus shipped `.aupreset` files generated from saved state. The duplication is deliberate and is a recorded wrapper deficit.

### Note input

One note input port supporting the CLAP, MIDI and MIDI-MPE dialects with CLAP preferred, which gains note ids where the host has them, and one stereo audio output port. Voices are matched on the full `(port, channel, key, note_id)` tuple with wildcards, so a host that issues an id only at note-on still releases the right voice. Note expressions `PRESSURE` and `EXPRESSION` feed key depth and per-voice pressure; `TUNING` is the per-note pitch input in semitones; `BRIGHTNESS` maps to the aperture scale; `VOLUME`, `PAN` and `VIBRATO` are mapped or ignored explicitly and the choice is doc-commented. Through clap-wrapper in Logic, channel pressure arrives as a channel-wide pressure expression with the key wildcarded, polyphonic key pressure arrives per key, and pitch bend arrives as a tuning expression at a fixed two semitones with no RPN handling; the plan accepts that, and glides in Logic go through the two-key mapping and the tuning tables rather than through bend. `NOTE_CHOKE` releases every reed of the voice immediately; `reset` and `deactivate` release everything.

### Tuning and continuous pitch

The first release is twelve-tone equal temperament at A 440 with the reference pitch as a parameter and the tuning ids reserved. The beyond-acoustic phase adds temperament tables, Gandhar tuning after Chimote and Oke's 22 shruti with per-key lower and upper selection, twelve per-key offsets, and per-note tuning expressions. Draft `clap.tuning` has no shipping host and is not implemented; an MTS-ESP client, which works in Logic but adds a C++ source to the Zig build, is a deferred issue.

Continuous pitch in the physics is not a frequency sweep. A reed's eigenfrequency, mass, stiffness, pumped section and aperture geometry are tied together by cantilever scaling, `f ∝ t / L²`, and `family.zig` defines a one-parameter family along that law so a glide moves every non-playing parameter consistently and stays inside the region Puranik found stable. A swept-frequency oracle vector is the gate. The keyboard-native path is the mapping West, Puranik, Scavone and Wanderley built into a sensor-retrofitted harmonium driving a synthesis model: the sounding pitch is interpolated between the two held keys, `p = (1 − w′) p₁ + w′ p₂` with `w′ = min(1, w₂ / w₁)` from the two key depths, and the amplitude follows the key-press weight. `p` is a pitch, so this path drives the same reed family as the glide, with the two keys supplying the target and the weight in place of a bend message. It works in Logic because polyphonic key pressure is delivered per key.

## The harness and the oracle

Physics discovery happens in Python with Numba, because every "what does this look like" costs a recompile in Zig and nothing in Zig plots. The harness lives in `harness/` as PEP 723 scripts with `#!/usr/bin/env -S uv run --script` shebangs and inline metadata pinning NumPy, SciPy, Numba and Matplotlib, following audio-tools rather than a `pyproject.toml`, with `ruff.toml` at the root and a `python` CI job as fosforo has. Numba constrains the Python version it supports; the scripts pin one that Numba supports at the time Phase 2 starts, and that pin is recorded.

The harness is not a parallel engine. ADR 0015 draws the line: Python discovers the single reed, its aperture, its solver and its internal rate, and once the Zig port of `src/model/reed.zig`, `aperture.zig`, `flow.zig` and `solver.zig` agrees with it, the Python single-reed model is frozen as the oracle for that seam. The reservoir, the multi-reed coupling, the banks and the cabinet are developed in Zig only, with `zig build render` as the instrument: an executable behind its own build step that takes a score and a bellows curve, renders a WAV and dumps the named state trajectories, and exits 0, 1 or 2; its bordered-Newton reference mode is the multi-reed reference the explicit coupling scheme is checked against. `compare.py` and `plot.py` consume its output. This is fosforo's harness-as-build-step pattern with a WAV instead of a screenshot, and it is also how the owner hears the model before the plugin can play it.

The oracle has two tiers and both carry controls:

- **Lockstep.** Python in fixed-K mode against Zig, both in `f64`, Zig in `.strict` float mode, the same `K` and bracket rule, the same aperture table and interpolation, the same lagged chamber update, the coefficients computed once and shared, a defined initial state and onset seed. The tolerance is set from the measured agreement and recorded; bit-exact is the target and the actual figure is stated.
- **Perceptual.** Fundamental frequency in cents, partial levels in decibels, growth rate in inverse seconds, the rising and falling thresholds, the onset pitch shift, and the audible decay time, each with a tolerance chosen from the corpus's own spread.

Every comparison rejects NaN explicitly, because NaN compares false against every bound and a blank render passes a bare range check, and every gate has a row in a plant table: a detuned reed, a wrong aperture table, a swapped flow derivative, each confirmed to turn the comparison red before the gate counts as coverage. Analytic invariants that need no recording sit beside them: Fletcher's threshold as a function of volume and Q, playing frequency below the eigenfrequency, `τ = Q / (π f₀)` for the free ring-down, and Helmholtz choking when a chamber resonance approaches the reed frequency. Shared constants between the harness and the Zig core are pinned by a Zig test that reads the Python source as text, the mechanism fosforo uses for `measure-trace`.

Phase 1 puts the Audio Unit first, as springer did, because an `aumu` instrument with note input through clap-wrapper is unmeasured by either sibling, and proving it against a placeholder sine finds a wrapper limitation before any reed code depends on it. The sine is throwaway and says so in its doc comment. Phase 2's harness lane needs no host and no exclusive resource, so it starts as soon as Phase 0 merges and runs beside Phase 1.

Phases 4, 5 and 6 together are the first playable instrument and Phase 7 ships it. Phase 4 and Phase 5 are separate because their gates are unlike: one is a planted-defect matrix and a latency measurement, the other is calibration against the corpus, and a sag figure outside its target and a hung note should fail as different things. The cabinet filter, the reservoir and the drones are all in the first release because the plan's own finding is that no earlier build sounds like a harmonium. Phase 8 precedes Phase 9 because the owner's target instrument has three banks and the particular sound depends on them, and because the modulation phase modulates every voice, so building it once the voice fan-out is complete avoids modulating a voice model that is still changing. The order of 8 and 9 is the one sequencing choice most likely to be revisited, and it can be, since neither depends on the other.

Issues are filed just in time, one milestone per phase, when a phase becomes the next one. A milestone marks what has to close before that phase's gate is met, not what is one of its numbered steps. Each issue lands its code and its verification together in one PR.

## Explicitly deferred

Recorded here so they are visibly refusals rather than omissions.

- **In-rank reed coupling and mode-locking.** Unsupported for the target instrument; the banks are sealed and octave-separated. Coupling through the shared chamber pressure is modelled because it falls out of the topology. A Male-Celeste bank with a detuned unison pair, with the accordion celeste range of 0.5 to 15 Hz beat rate as its target, is the eventual home if one is ever wanted.
- **The hybrid signal-model engine.** DAFx-23's wavetable through biquads is a different instrument, not a cheaper version of this one. It reopens only if the CPU gate fails at the chosen polyphony.
- **The modal and clamped-bar reeds.** The reed seam is sized for them. They arrive when the single-mode reed's high-pressure deficit is measured against the corpus and found to matter.
- **MTS-ESP and draft `clap.tuning`.** The first adds C++ to the build; the second has no host. Internal tables and per-note tuning expressions come first.
- **A dipole radiation model.** The monopole approximation ships on both channels; the dipole is judged once the filter and the recordings exist.
- **The keyboard beyond 42 keys.** The acoustic instrument's range is an affordance, not a limit of the model; extending it is a Phase 9 or later decision with its own reed-table consequence.
- **VST3, AUv3, AAX, standalone.** A later clap-wrapper toggle, not a design problem.
- **Sample playback of any kind, waveguides, the European harmonium and the American reed organ.** ADR 0019. They differ in reed geometry, rank count and air supply, and modelling them would change what the plugin is.
- **The GUI.** ADR 0020. When it arrives it is native AppKit controls or a Metal-rendered panel, decided then. Whether it ever shows state-variable plots, which would reserve fosforo's ring and display-link machinery, is decided then too; nothing is reserved now.

## Risks

- **Solver stability under parameter sweeps.** Puranik's adaptation of this model needed unrealistic values to stay stable and per-note retuning of the non-playing parameters. Mitigations: the regularised jet, the bracketed solver, the reed family, and a stability sweep over pressure from zero to 1.5 kPa and over the family as a Phase 2 oracle vector. Status: open until Phase 2 measures it.
- **The CPU gate at the chosen polyphony.** The estimate straddles the target. Mitigations, in preference order and all decided in Phase 2 where the lane figure, `K` and the rate are still open: lanes across reeds, a smaller `K`, a lower internal rate, and last the hybrid engine. After Phase 2 freezes `K` and the rate, changing either is an amendment to ADR 0010 or 0011 and a regeneration of the oracle vectors, which CI turns red until it happens, so no such change is quiet. Status: open until Phase 2 measures the per-reed cost.
- **Calibration without an instrument.** Absolute aperture geometry, the side clearance and reed damping are uncertain; the corpus carries no pressure and mostly unknown instruments. Mitigations: the single absolute anchor (FA2023's B5 reed), the pressure trace, the Q bracket, the analytic invariants, and treating the uncertain constants as parameters with documented sensitivity. Status: accepted.
- **The reservoir's sag is an untested inference.** No published model has more than one reed drawing on one volume. Mass conservation makes it plausible, and the repair literature's hold and leak-down times are the calibration targets. Status: open until Phase 5.
- **The near-field topology is unmeasured.** The pallet-downstream residual carries two square-root terms and no published model has solved it; the pipe placement and the chamber-volume scaling are Phase 2 findings. Status: open until Phase 2.
- **`aumu` with note input through clap-wrapper.** The build helper honours the explicit type, PR #493 added the MIDI path, and neither sibling exercised an instrument. Status: resolved by the Phase 1 Logic gate on 2026-09-16 with Logic Pro 12.3.1 on macOS 26.6.2: instrument insertion, stereo held-key sine, release silence and repeated-note playback passed.
- **Bitwig is a new host for this family.** Nothing in fosforo's host notes covers it. Its licence, its CLAP behaviour and its voice stack are all Phase 3 findings. Status: open.
- **`auval` will probably never see this plugin.** A clap-wrapper property the siblings measured; Logic is the only complete check. Status: accepted.
- **gh-actions release timing.** `set-up-clap-validator` is on `main` and unreleased. Phase 1 consumes it from the first release that ships it, and copies fosforo's composite action as the documented fallback if that release has not been cut. Status: resolved on 2026-09-14, when `cboone/gh-actions` v3.2.0 released `set-up-clap-validator`.
- **Parameter count in Logic's generic list.** The first release carries about fifty parameters and the full instrument well over a hundred, which is the strongest argument for pulling the GUI forward from Phase 10. `module` paths and AUv2 ordering are the mitigation until then. Status: accepted.
- **Numba lags Python releases.** The harness pins a Python version Numba supports, and that pin is a scheduled update, not an accident. Status: accepted.
- **Zig 0.17 will move things.** Runtime vector indexing is already forbidden in 0.16, the float-mode builtin is slated for removal in favour of typed reals, and translate-c changed backends. Scoping the float mode per file and coercing vectors to arrays are the migration-safe idioms. Status: accepted.

## Verification

End to end, once Phase 7 lands:

```bash
zig build                                    # assembles zig-out/Savera.clap
zig fmt --check build.zig src/
zig build test                               # unit tests, canaries, seam, feature string, bindings
zig build test-safe && zig build test-release
zig build render -- fixtures/scores/a3-sustain.json fixtures/bellows/steady-500pa.csv verification/a3.wav
harness/compare.py verification/a3.wav fixtures/renders/a3-sustain.npz
zig build smoke                              # the offline host harness
zig build validate                           # clap-validator over the Zig-built bundle
scripts/build-audio-unit                     # build/assets/Savera.component, via CMake
plutil -extract AudioComponents.0.type raw build/assets/Savera.component/Contents/Info.plist
clap-validator validate build/assets/Savera.clap
auval -v aumu Svra Ctmn                      # expected to find nothing; see Risks
zig build --release=fast install-plugins     # both bundles into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins
scripts/read-provenance --check ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/Savera.clap
```

Then, by hand, on a software instrument track, after confirming the provenance of the installed build. Each step is written out in full in the phase plan that introduces it, in the Setup, Action, Expected, Null-versus-broken form; the summaries below name the property, not the procedure.

1. In Logic, play a key at a fixed bellows value and confirm a note sounds, swells with the expression pedal, and neither pitch nor onset depends on velocity.
2. Hold a chord of three keys and confirm the reservoir sags: the level drops and recovers on the next pump stroke, and a single held note outlasts the chord. The null arm is the same chord with the reservoir spring set to its stiffest value, where the sag must vanish.
3. Press a key partway with polyphonic aftertouch or a per-note pressure expression and confirm the note comes in quieter, fades in, and sits slightly flat. The null arm is full depth, where none of the three happens.
4. Open two drones and confirm they sound continuously, draw on the same reservoir, and sag with the keyboard notes.
5. Release a bass note and a treble note together and confirm the bass rings longer. The null arm is the same pair with the Q scale at its minimum, where both stop together.
6. Switch the cabinet between the jali presets and confirm the top end changes as the India Instruments demos do.
7. Set the pump to tempo-synced and confirm the strokes lock to the transport and stop when it stops.
8. Learn a non-default controller to Bellows and confirm the note swells with it and the parameter still reads it after the transport stops; in REAPER and Bitwig, record in touch mode and confirm no parameter automation is written for the learned controller, recording any host that writes it anyway as a host refusal, since the Audio Unit path never sees the flag.
9. Save the project, reopen it, and confirm every parameter returns, the learned controller assignments return with them, and no modulation amount was persisted.
10. Confirm the automation lane binds to the right parameters after a rebuild, which is the check that the stable-id scheme is actually working.
11. Bounce a region that ends on a released bass note and a held drone, and record what the ring-down and the drone do under the Audio Unit's missing tail.
12. In REAPER, load the CLAP, confirm the factory presets appear through preset discovery, and confirm parameter modulation through its own modulators changes the sound and that `IS_LIVE` knob turns are not recorded.
13. In Bitwig, add a per-note modulator on the aperture scale and confirm each note is modulated independently, that the voice stack allocates one voice per note, and that released voices are freed when the reed has rung down.
